# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Our Standards
Participants should be respectful, constructive, and considerate.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and activities.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior may result in appropriate corrective action.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers will review reported incidents and take suitable action.

## Attribution
This Code of Conduct is inspired by community standards used in open-source projects.
